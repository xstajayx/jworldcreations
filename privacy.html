Privacy

This page describes privacy practices for the public website at jworldcreations.com.

Basic technical information (such as standard web server logs) may be processed by hosting providers to operate and secure the website.

JWorldCreations is responsible for personal information it handles through this site. See Business Information for contact details. The site has no account registration or contact form. If you email us, we use your address and the content you send to respond and handle follow-up. Our legitimate interests are responding to enquiries and operating and securing the site.

Individual products, apps, or services linked from this website may have their own privacy notices where applicable. Please review those directly when using a specific product.

GitHub Pages hosts the site and Microsoft hosts the support mailbox. These services can process information outside the UK. We keep correspondence while needed to deal with an enquiry or meet legal obligations. Email us to ask about retention or transfer safeguards, or to exercise applicable access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability rights. Requests are normally free. You can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.
